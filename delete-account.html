Delete your Doggo account

Doggo (published by Paulius Masalskas) · Last updated: 7 September 2026

Delete your account from inside the app

This is the fastest way, and it removes everything immediately. It works on both Android and iOS.

  1. Open the Doggo app and make sure you are signed in.
  2. On the map screen, tap your dog in the bottom bar to open My Pack.
  3. Tap the … (more) button in the top-right corner, then tap Settings.
  4. Scroll down to the Account section and tap Delete Account.
  5. Read the summary, tap Delete my account, then confirm in the alert.

Your account and data are erased straight away and you are signed out. There is no waiting period and no way to undo it.

Delete your account by email

If you cannot sign in or cannot reach the screen above, email 0xpaulius@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my Doggo account”. Tell us your dog’s name so we can confirm we are deleting the right account.

We will confirm your identity, delete the account, and reply to confirm within 30 days (usually within a few days).

What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently erases all of the following:

  • Your account and sign-in records (Google or Apple sign-in link, sessions, tokens) and your email address
  • Your dog profiles, dog photos and cover photos, and your owner photo
  • Your app preferences and onboarding answers
  • Your saved spots, wishlists and collections
  • Your paw ratings, reviews and check-ins
  • Your posts, journal entries, photos and comments
  • Photos you added to places
  • Your walks, walk routes, distances and walk photos
  • Your friends, friend requests and blocks
  • Reports you filed about other content

Files stored for you (photos and images) are deleted from our file storage at the same time as the database records.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Safety records — reports and moderation flags filed by other users about your content are kept as an anonymised safety record, so that reports about still-active accounts are not lost. They no longer identify you. Retained for up to 2 years.
  • Purchase and subscription records — if you bought a Doggo subscription, the transaction records held by Google Play, Apple and our billing provider (RevenueCat) are retained for tax and accounting purposes as required by law, typically up to 7 years. Cancel a subscription in the Google Play or App Store subscription settings; deleting your Doggo account does not cancel it.
  • Encrypted backups — copies of your data may remain in our provider’s encrypted backups for a short period after deletion and are overwritten within 30 days.

Nothing else is retained. We do not keep a shadow copy of your profile, posts, photos or walks.

Delete some of your data without deleting your account

You do not have to delete your whole account to remove things. Inside the app you can delete individual posts and journal entries, comments, photos you added to places, saved spots and collections, and dog profiles.

For anything you cannot remove yourself, email 0xpaulius@gmail.com describing what you want removed. We will action it and reply within 30 days.
